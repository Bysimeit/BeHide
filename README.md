# BeHide
*Who has never dreamed of being able to discuss with the certainty that nothing will ever be disclosed?*\
***That’s what we promise you***
## Our Promises
* No data will be saved on a server
* The encryption keys are internal to your devices, at no time is it possible to spy on you
* Message backups can only be done with your consent and will never be stored anywhere other than on your device
* Everything to guarantee security, including opening the code
## What can we expect?
> For the moment, classic secure messaging app. But we'll see in the future depending to your enthusiasm
